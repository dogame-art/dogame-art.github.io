About the Artist

Dogame is a digital artist and illustrator working across the intersection of traditional art sensibility and on-chain publishing. With a style rooted in surreal landscapes, expressive characters, and vivid color, the work explores themes of solitude, curiosity, and the spaces between worlds.

My goal is for art to be accessible — something you can enjoy even at your cubicle. Every piece is a window into a world that feels just a little different from this one.

Work is available on Drip Haus.
